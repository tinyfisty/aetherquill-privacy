Aetherquill Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Aetherquill (“Aetherquill,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an AI-powered role-playing game that allows players to interact with and shape personalized fictional adventures.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, and protected when you use Aetherquill.

Information We Collect

Gameplay Content

When you use Aetherquill, you may provide open-ended text and other gameplay input, including character actions, dialogue choices, descriptions, preferences, and responses within your adventure.

Aetherquill collects and processes this content to operate the game, generate responses, maintain narrative context, and personalize your gameplay experience.

Gameplay Actions

Aetherquill may collect information about actions you take within the game, such as gameplay choices, dialogue selections, character decisions, and other interactions that affect your adventure.

Information You Voluntarily Include

Because Aetherquill allows open-ended text input, you may choose to include information about yourself in your gameplay input.

You should avoid including personal, confidential, or sensitive information that is not necessary to use the game.

Aetherquill does not currently require users to create an account or provide a real name, email address, phone number, physical address, payment information, or precise location in order to play.

How We Use Information

Information collected by Aetherquill is used to:

  • provide and operate the game's features;
  • process player actions and generate appropriate game responses;
  • maintain the context and continuity of an adventure; and
  • personalize gameplay based on player input, choices, and previous events.

Aetherquill does not use gameplay information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Artificial Intelligence Processing

Aetherquill uses artificial intelligence services to generate and support interactive game experiences.

Information necessary to process a player's action or generate the adventure may be transmitted to service providers that process information on Aetherquill's behalf.

These service providers may process gameplay input and relevant game context as necessary to provide their services to Aetherquill and subject to their applicable data-processing terms.

Aetherquill currently uses OpenAI technology for AI processing. OpenAI states that, by default, inputs and outputs submitted through its API platform are not used to train or improve OpenAI's models unless the API customer explicitly opts in to data sharing.

Sharing of Information

Aetherquill does not sell users' personal information.

Aetherquill does not share user information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be transmitted to service providers acting on Aetherquill's behalf when necessary to operate the service, including artificial-intelligence or technical infrastructure providers.

We may also disclose information when required by law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of Aetherquill, its users, or others.

Analytics and Advertising

The current version of Aetherquill does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics services designed to track user activity for advertising or marketing purposes.

Aetherquill does not currently collect advertising identifiers for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Security

Aetherquill uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by the service.

User data transmitted between the application and supporting services is encrypted in transit using secure network protocols.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Gameplay information may be retained as necessary to provide the game experience, maintain adventure context, operate Aetherquill, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

Aetherquill is currently in development, and its data-retention practices may evolve as persistent campaigns, user accounts, and other features are introduced. This Privacy Policy will be updated when those practices materially change.

Data Deletion

Aetherquill does not currently provide user accounts or an automated in-app data-deletion feature.

Questions concerning information associated with Aetherquill may be directed to the privacy contact listed below.

As account-management and data-deletion functionality is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the available deletion options.

Children's Privacy

Aetherquill is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected contrary to the intended use of the service, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

Third-Party Services

Aetherquill may rely on third-party service providers to operate portions of the service, including artificial-intelligence processing and technical infrastructure.

Those providers may process information on Aetherquill's behalf subject to their applicable terms and privacy or data-processing practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Aetherquill develops, including when new features, account systems, service providers, or data practices are introduced.

When the policy is materially updated, the effective date at the top of this page will be revised.
